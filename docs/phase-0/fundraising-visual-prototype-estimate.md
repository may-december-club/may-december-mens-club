# Fundraising Visual Prototype — Commercial Estimate

Date: 2026-09-08
Status: **ESTIMATE ONLY — EXECUTION NOT AUTHORIZED**

## Scope basis

This estimate is based on the approved Fundraising Visual Prototype BRD v1.0 dated 2026-09-07. The prototype is separate scope from Phase 0 and separate from the future full Production build.

The deliverable is a high-fidelity clickable responsive-web prototype for crowdfunding, investors and prospective members. It does not include a Production backend/database, live member data, Production authentication, live ID verification/OTP, live search/messaging, payments, moderation queues, admin systems, analytics, live AI or native applications.

The controlled BRD requires 10 core screens, journey/screen inventory, low-fidelity wireframes, visual direction, high-fidelity screens, clickable prototype, responsive anchor states, presentation exports, editable source files, component/design-system guidance, PRD traceability and asset/license inventory, with seven Product Owner approval gates.

## Commercial estimate

- Human-effort planning range: **87–112h**.
- Planning midpoint: **approximately 100h**.
- Planning rate basis: **BRL 150/h**.
- Range at rate basis: **BRL 13,050–16,800**.
- Recommended fixed-fee proposal: **BRL 15,000** for the controlled BRD v1.0 scope.
- Expected elapsed calendar: **3–4 weeks**, assuming timely Product Owner review at the seven approval gates; approximately 3 weeks is possible only with fast review/decision turnaround.

The fixed-fee assumption covers normal correction/conformance iterations required to meet the approved BRD. Product Owner-requested scope changes after a gate approval, additional screens/journeys, Production functionality or new deliverable classes require separate written change approval.

## AI/design-tool and third-party costs

AI-assisted design/drafting is expected as a delivery accelerator under Technical Lead direction and human review. No dedicated paid AI/design-tool purchase is committed by this estimate unless separately approved.

Planning allowance for incremental licensed assets/design-tool costs: **BRL 0–1,500**, only if required and approved before purchase. Third-party stock, font, icon, illustration or other licensed assets must be inventoried and their usage rights confirmed. Existing approved/business-owned tools or assets should be reused where appropriate.

## Assumptions

- Controlling product source remains Master PRD v2.0 approved through Section 12.
- Existing public brand is reference and the approved purple/white visual direction is retained.
- Prototype interactions are simulated; no live Production services are required.
- No Browse All Members or swipe-style mechanics are introduced.
- Verification/profile-completion rules, trust/safety principles and Premium safety boundaries are represented consistently with the BRD.
- Product Owner supplies review decisions promptly at each gate.
- Human review remains mandatory for BRD conformance, interaction consistency, responsive behavior and presentation readiness.

## Exclusions

Production backend/database, live member accounts/data, Production auth/IDV/OTP, real search/messaging/payments, moderation/admin queues, analytics, live AI, native apps, Production infrastructure, security certification, penetration testing, legal/privacy review for Production, crowdfunding campaign operations and future full-build implementation are excluded.

## Authorization boundary

This document satisfies the requested **estimate**. It does not authorize prototype execution. No billable prototype work, material provider purchase, licensed-asset commitment or third-party engagement should begin without explicit written Product Owner authorization.
